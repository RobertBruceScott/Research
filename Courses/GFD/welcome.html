Présentation

La Geophysical Fluid Dynamics is a cours for students of M2 Physics at UBO.

Programme du Master Physique – UBO

My role is to lead the tutorial classes in this course. The lectures are given by my colleague Xavier Carton.

Supporting material for the tutorials

Tutorials (travaux dirigés)

  • TD 1: Exercise 5. Coriolis acceleration and the Wilhelm Reich experiment (Pont Albert Louppe experiment!).
  • TD 2: Exercise 1. Hydrostatic versus dynamic pressure, Exercise 2. Mid latitude winds (geostrophy), Exercise 3. Thermal wind shear.
  • TD 2: Supplement. Exercise 1. Coriolis parameter and Earth's rotation, and Furevik's Notes, Section 5: exercises on Coriolis acceleration.
